# Bind closed-loop main capital separately from nominal expert intentions

The four-expert diagnosis shows that an expert's shadow protection does not control
the combined main capital account. This research keeps the complete experts and
their registered nominal intentions unchanged, but tests the existing ratchet law
at a new Seam using each main account's own realized net wealth and peak.

Pre-outcome registration binds the immutable nominal inputs and causal transition
Implementation, not a fictional precomputed sequence of state-dependent issued
targets. Those targets are produced once with each newly funded path after its
publication fence and checked against independent scalar state and BT Adapters.
Using source shadow wealth instead would avoid the closed loop but answer a
different question; running a main book before publication merely to precompute
its targets would violate the research sequence. Daily inspection is an explicit
policy change, not a claim that the old monthly rule or its failures were repaired.
