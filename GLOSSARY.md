# Portfolio research

This context studies causal, cash-funded portfolios and preserves the distinction
between usable research evidence and a portfolio that meets its final objective.

## Language

**Base factor (基础因子)**:
An economic signal or exposure such as momentum, value, quality or low volatility.
It is not a complete portfolio policy.
_Avoid_: Strategy expert, asset sleeve

**Strategy expert (子策略专家)**:
A complete causal portfolio policy with its own information requirements and
allocation behavior. A valid expert may fail the final portfolio's performance gates.
_Avoid_: Factor when referring to an entire strategy, asset renamed as an expert

**Expert availability (专家可用性)**:
The ability to obtain an admissible recommendation from an expert at a particular
decision time. It does not imply that enough observations exist to score the expert.
_Avoid_: Qualification, future availability

**Source admission scope (来源准入范围)**:
The information and decision contexts for which a source has accepted evidence of
admissibility. Approval for a restricted use does not admit every historical query,
derived view or strategy expert using that source.
_Avoid_: Blanket source approval, final portfolio qualification

**Score maturity (评分成熟度)**:
The availability, at a decision time, of all observations required by a specified
expert assessment. An unobserved or incomplete assessment is not a successful score.
_Avoid_: Filled score, safe default

**Assessment admissibility (评估可行性)**:
Satisfaction of a combination rule's stated constraints on observations known at a
decision. It does not establish an expert's validity or final portfolio qualification.
_Avoid_: Final qualification, future risk guarantee

**Expert introduction (专家引入)**:
The actual time an expert is defined and admitted into research. It is distinct
from the dates on which a historical counterfactual assumes its policy was available.
_Avoid_: Backdated introduction, independent historical deployment

**Mechanism group (机制组)**:
A group of experts with closely related economic logic or risk behavior.
Multiple variants in a group are not evidence of multiple independent risk sources.
_Avoid_: Independent factor count

**Risk cluster (风险聚类)**:
A group of strategies formed from their observed joint risk behavior for allocation.
Its membership does not establish a new economic factor or independent source of returns.
_Avoid_: Mechanism group, independent factor family

**Shadow book (影子账簿)**:
A separate record used to observe and assess an expert's behavior.
Its wealth and costs are not capital or realized performance of the final portfolio.
_Avoid_: Main capital account, transferable wealth guarantee

**Meta-strategy (元策略)**:
A causal policy for combining complete strategy experts into a unified portfolio.
It is evaluated on the resulting portfolio, not on averages of expert summary metrics.
_Avoid_: Best historical winner, averaged CAGR

**Allocation learning state (分配学习状态)**:
The accumulated assessment information a meta-strategy uses to revise future capital
weights. It is not the main account's holdings, cash or realized wealth.
_Avoid_: Main capital account, transferable shadow wealth guarantee

**Contest-credit forecast (竞赛积分预期)**:
An assessment of how a later period's winning credit may be divided among complete
strategy experts. It is not expected portfolio return, deployable capital or final
qualification.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed winner, expected main-account return

**Expert persistence prior (专家持续性先验)**:
A declared assumption about how assessment weight persists or is refreshed across
complete strategy experts. It is not a measured market-transition probability or
a guarantee of lasting performance.
_Avoid_: Observed market regime, calibrated expert-success probability

**Expert reversal forecast (专家反转预期)**:
An expectation that recent relative weakness among complete experts will reverse.
It is an allocation premise, not an observed recovery or an independent source of returns.
_Avoid_: Realized rebound, expert qualification, guaranteed recovery

**Market context (市场情境)**:
An observable joint market condition used to distinguish expert assessments.
It is not a latent economic regime, a causal explanation or an independent return source.
_Avoid_: Proven macro regime, hindsight regime label

**Co-loss assessment (共损评估)**:
An assessment of an expert's nonoffsetting loss contribution on dates when a specified
source mixture is losing. It is not the final portfolio's loss or a drawdown guarantee.
_Avoid_: Main-account risk guarantee, hedge-adjusted realized loss

**Assessment-to-main divergence (评估—主账户偏离)**:
A difference between assessment reference performance and main-account performance
over comparable periods. It does not by itself identify an implementation defect or a
causal source of loss.
_Avoid_: Transferable shadow return, isolated fee effect

**Funding standby (资金备用状态)**:
A declared mode that parks main capital when the meta-strategy cannot form an
admissible expert allocation. It is not a strategy expert or a successful risk assessment.
_Avoid_: Additional expert, guaranteed safe allocation

**Main capital account (主资金账户)**:
The single pool of model capital whose asset holdings, cash, execution and costs
determine a formal portfolio path.
_Avoid_: Sum of shadow books, brokerage account

**Drawdown headroom (回撤余量)**:
The amount by which current main wealth is above or below the wealth implied by a
specified loss limit relative to its retained peak. It is not idle cash, available
buying power or a guarantee against a later breach.
_Avoid_: Cash balance, guaranteed protection

**Stress reference loss (压力参照损失)**:
The loss assigned to a portfolio under an observed or specified stress scenario.
It is not a forecast of the next return or a guaranteed maximum future loss.
_Avoid_: Realized main loss, future drawdown guarantee

**Final portfolio qualification (最终组合资格)**:
Joint satisfaction of every required performance and operating constraint across
all formal paths of the same frozen portfolio rule.
_Avoid_: Expert admission, one passing metric, independent forward proof
