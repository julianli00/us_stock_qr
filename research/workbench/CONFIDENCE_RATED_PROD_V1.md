# Confidence-rated six-expert Prod V1

## Frozen question and prior art

Protective membership produced little allocation influence, while fixed economic
role budgets did not satisfy joint financial gates. This study changes the
conditional learning game, not another capital floor, window or rate coefficient.
It retains the same six admitted complete policies, original monthly master
execution and all original financial gates.

Daily equal-eight native-opinion consumption and actual-main wealth ratchets
already exist and were evaluated. This is neither their repetition nor a claim
that a source-owned protection floor transfers to main capital.

## Learner and confidence clock

Use Gaillard, Stoltz and van Erven, COLT 2014, PMLR 35:176-196,
[A second-order bound with excess losses](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v35/gaillard14.html).
Algorithm 2 and Corollary 4 define Adapt-ML-Prod; equation 9 and Proposition 7
give the confidence-rated reduction. The original PDF pages 5-10 were read
before new learning. Public math is summarized here; raw paper content stays
private on the SSD.

Start six raw masses at one sixth. Each expert's rate is the smaller of one half
and the square root of log(6) divided by one plus its past squared confidence
excess losses. The half bound is the paper's positive-update condition, not a
fitted economic cap. Current capital fractions normalize confidence times rate
times raw mass. After observing the unchanged bounded source losses, compute
the played loss first. Expert excess is confidence times played-minus-source
loss. Update its squared excess, derive the next rate, then raise old mass times
one-plus-old-rate-times-excess to the next-rate/old-rate power.
**Do not renormalize the six raw masses after updating:** unequal powers make
that a different algorithm. Actual capital fractions are normalized separately.

The five nonfeedback policies participate with rating one. The existing
wealth-feedback source uses one minus its original clipped protection scale,
derived from its saved own-shadow NAV and prefix peak, including initial
USD10000. This asymmetric mapping is one preregistered research choice about
specialization. It is not a calibrated probability, source-admission judgment,
portfolio-risk certificate or guarantee. Zero participation preserves the
complete admitted expert and its learning state; it is not technical exclusion.
The other five positive masses always keep the normalization well-defined.

The rating recorded at the prior source-month origin governs that month's
loss update. A new end-month rating cannot retroactively select which past
losses count. Current ratings govern only the next allocation. Five-year
capital starts fresh but carries the original expanding source-learning history;
it does not reset masses or invent another October 5 source refresh.
Initial October 5 and October 31 decisions have no completed learning month.
Their allocation is the uniform raw prior after confidence normalization,
not necessarily equal sixths and not a successful score.
