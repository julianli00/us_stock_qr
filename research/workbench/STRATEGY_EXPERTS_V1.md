## Two different gates

Expert admission concerns admitted sources, information available at each
decision, reproducible recommendations, correct capital accounting and permitted
long-only, cash-funded exposure. Standalone Sharpe, benchmark or drawdown failure
does not itself exclude an expert. Technical failures, leakage, unadmitted data or
prohibited leverage cannot be made valid by blending them.

Distinguish admitted representatives, admissible variants not selected for the
first pool, unreviewed archives, and demonstrated invalid/archive-only versions.
Do not label unreviewed history invalid. Deduplicate closely related mechanisms
and risk behavior without claiming that the remaining experts are independent
or complementary before measuring their joint behavior.
