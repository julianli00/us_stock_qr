# Signed style activity participation: primary V1

This prepares one complete monthly policy for a possible complementary
cross-sectional price/activity participation role in an expert pool. It is not a
new independent economic factor, financial winner, admitted strategy expert or
automatic pool member. All271 prior financial completions and the new loss
diagnosis are exposed; preparation does not establish historical performance.

Only MTUM/VLUE/QUAL/USMV have the exact reused reported-activity admission in
this study. IJR/PKW and other price-admitted funds are not silently admitted as
activity inputs. The existing native-CSV/source proof, positive integer share
volumes, zero reported splits, source scope and original price tolerances remain.
The old source verifier is not rerun and no new provider response is requested.

For each covered genuine NYSE month endD, take63daily adjusted-close directions
endingD-1, requiring64prior adjusted closes. Weight each direction (-1/0/+1) by
its same-ending-day reported raw close times reported share volume and divide
by their sum. Scaling activity by its positive maximum is numerical only.
There is no clipping, missing-data fill or degenerate-score cash fallback.
A flat observed price sequence has a genuinely defined zero balance.

At least three of the four defined balances must be strictly positive.
Otherwise request98%actualBIL. When at least three are positive, rank them by
12-decimal balance then ticker, and allocate98%equally to the top three.
The fourteen-asset output is complete with2%idle cash. The quarter-length
window, one-session lag, three-style participation and zero sign threshold are
single predeclared research choices, not user requirements or a parameter grid.

The small policy Module exposes a numerical balance, complete allocation,
one causal opinion and a sparse native target builder. Native opinions are
genuine month ends only; a formal account anchor observes a prior opinion
instead of inventing an October5policy event. Price warmup is not NET-score
maturity, and actual future registration/admission is not backdated.

Prior activity momentum weighted105log-return magnitudes, skipped21recent days,
kept a parent policy's four-style budget and35/30/20/15rank shares, and was
already tested in a paired expert study. The SPY impact source used absolute
return per reported activity to change broad participation. This new policy
uses a signed direction fraction on four styles to choose its own complete
style-or-BIL budget; it does not retune either old parent, ratio or failed score.
These related price/activity transforms are not independent risk families.
Any future complete-policy comparison would not isolate causal volume alpha.

Close times reported volume is a closing-valuation activity proxy, not executed
dollars, investor inflows, buyer-initiated flow or stock-market breadth.
ETF structure, distributions, units and price direction can confound it.
The declared lag does not prove first historical publication or revision-free PIT.

The input Adapter consumes only the admitted four-fund price/activity fields
and checks compatibility with the current frozen fourteen-asset context.
It does NOT import the earlier policy's RF or create an assessment bridge.
This new policy's own future accounts must use the unchanged current canonical
`review_market` RF; no claim of old-native-RF equality or source waiver follows.

This primary stage freezes code/tests/source evidence and then checks source
compatibility and calendar readiness only. No real balance, rank, opinion,
target, shadow, account or performance is computed. A separate financial program,
actual registration before real outputs, full publication before four original
freshUSD10000paths, independent accounting/BT and a technical expert review are
still required. The original costs, timing, RF, initial peak, all gates, failures
and global seven/eight rosters remain unchanged.
