# Current-opinion covariance risk calibration V1

This risk-only study asks whether a frozen current-opinion mark scenario
describes subsequent statistical expert risk differently from trailing expert
NET covariance. It precedes, and does not authorize or automatically select,
any new allocator. The four complete source policies and both recorded outer
weight tapes remain unchanged.

The source is the closed growth/macro/defense/hedge quartet, not four asset
sleeves. Its growth controller contains older canonical policies; distinct
labels do not establish independence. Actual source introduction/admission
clocks and the earlier counterfactual historical-availability scope remain.
Existing assessment RF views are consumed unchanged, not declared identical
to every original source's native RF. No new RF view or source waiver is made.

## Frozen comparison

At each of the original 121 initial/month-end origins, retain the exact four
known opinions and equal/recorded-rolling fractions. Require 126 complete
mature return sessions ending at that origin. The six-month history is
inherited, not selected from a grid. Use sample covariance with ddof=1.

1. Trailing expert NET-RF covariance uses the four preserved source policy
   return series, including their historical state changes and costs.
2. Current-opinion gross-mark-RF covariance maps the same 126 admitted ETF
   return rows through the four current 98%-invested opinions, with 2% idle
   cash and the original full-capital assessment RF. It deliberately omits
   future policy updates, fees and actual main-account execution.

The latter is not a NET-return forecast or a replacement accounting model.
Both forecasts use only mature history and known opinions. The real Module
Interface separates `forecast` from `assess`, so future observations cannot
enter a forecast through a label argument. Synthetic and saved-data Adapters
cross this same Seam. Five exact forecast-prefix checks and independent scalar
variance checks verify Locality without claiming retrospective labels causal.

For each fixed outer view, the label is variance over the next 21 consecutive
source NET-RF observations with those statistical fractions held fixed.
Twenty-one sessions is a declared approximate trading month, not an optimized
holding period. Source policies may continue updating internally. These labels
are statistical mixtures, not funded portfolios, tradable performance, main
holdings or sums of shadow wealth.

Keep all origins. Unmatured histories are explicitly unscored, never cash or
successful-risk fallbacks. An incomplete terminal 21-session horizon retains
its forecast without a label; do not fill, shorten or splice the horizon.
Report absolute/signed variance errors, underforecast counts and nullable
forecast/realized variance ratios. No epsilon replaces zero realized variance.
Compare both models for both fixed views without selecting a winner, fitting
parameters from labels, changing a financial test or making a significance claim.

## Scope and persistence

The preceding accounting findings motivated this question after historical
outcomes were exposed. This is not independent out-of-sample or forward proof,
and overlapping 21-session labels and two related outer views are not
independent economic trials.

Freeze source references, Implementation, fixtures, numeric checks and design
before new real forecasts or labels. Preserve partial failures and stream
private input/resolution evidence. Publish only aggregate calibration results,
not source prices, daily returns, targets, private tapes or covariance inputs.

No target builder, old study entrypoint, policy fit, new source request, shadow,
main account, BT, benchmark, CAGR, Sharpe or qualification is run. Existing
financial rejections and all original full-window/net-cost/cash-funded/1x
acceptance gates remain. Financial J1320/289 combined completions/zero
qualifiers does not change; the investment objective remains unmet.
