# Dyadic source-interval meta-learning V1

This independently versioned financial question is not a repair of the
terminal inventory-scenario V1, a second-order log-growth repeat, a contextual
contest repeat, or a gain/hazard/window sweep. Six complete admitted policies
remain the only strategy experts. Interval learners are auxiliary allocation
memories, not new factors, strategies, independent trials or forward evidence.

## Fixed temporal law

Only the original119complete source months advance the learning clock.
The partial October2016initial month does not train. Source forecasts occur
at the original anchor and true NYSE month ends. Source history is shared;
the five-year main account starts with fresh10000USD and its own10000USDpeak,
without resetting the source learner or treating initialization as a refresh.

Lengths1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128form eight shifted calendar partitions. After
tcomplete labels, the active interval at lengthL starts at1+floor(t/L)*L
and endsL-1rounds later. New intervals start with zero local loss/regret;
expired interval states are retained in the chronological artifact. The
global source clock and played loss are never reset. Length128 is the next
power of two above the declared119-month source dataset, not a chosen winner.

Each interval forecasts the same six sources using exponential cumulative
bounded source loss and the fixed rate sqrt(8log(6)/L), the classical
finite-interval Hedge loss-bound minimizer. Rates are tied to calendar lengths,
not fitted market parameters or independent evaluated configurations.

The outer score is the unchanged scalar shifted-regret potential multiplied
by1/[s(s+1)L(L+1)]. That explicit summable calendar prior requires no selected
switch probability. Meta regret uses the previously played aggregate source
loss minus that interval's previously forecast loss; magnitude accumulates
its absolute increments. Invalid evidence raises an error. All-zero scores
use explicitly logged normalized active priors, without resetting states.
There is no weight floor or regret clipping.

The primary320-digit cumulative-loss/epoch recursion is checked against
160-digit multiplicative base evidence and independently recomputed epoch
regrets using its own prequential played-loss history. Positive scalar
potential primitives are reused, not old learners or completed entrypoints.
Original binary64 source labels and normalization remain unchanged.
Every new source-label inlet rejects boolean and textual values before
numerical conversion, including mixed lists accepted by a legacy helper.

Daniely,Gonen,Shalev-Shwartz(2015),*Strongly Adaptive Online Learning*,
PMLR37:1405-1411 motivates geometric interval calendars. Its public PDF is
captured privately with actual acquisition evidence. This hybrid is
research-owned: no faithful SAOL algorithm, strongly adaptive regret guarantee,
independent source-game validation or main-wealth/drawdown theorem is claimed.
