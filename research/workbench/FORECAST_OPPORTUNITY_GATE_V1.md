# Feasibility-first retained-forecast participation V1

This is a new allocation-participation law, not a patch of either rejected
forecast portfolio or a change to the15%main drawdown criterion. The retained
advisors and their eight complete experts, original forecasts, ranking, pair
ERC weights and previous statistical state remain immutable.

The mechanical diagnosis found many negative post-cost active-rate numerators.
The new question is whether requiring a **strictly positive nominal source
opportunity** before deploying an advisor can improve the joint main-account
objective. Zero is the economic boundary between positive and nonpositive
assessment, not a fitted DD/exposure threshold or parameter sweep.
Both unconditional/contextual models are included without picking a winner.
This highly restrictive rule may destroy benchmark returns; it is not assumed
to work.

## Interface and modes

`gate` accepts the joined saved advisor output and its same-date exact-key sign
certificate, and returns one14asset target per new policy plus explicit mode
evidence. Cached-record and synthetic-record Adapters cross this Interface.
Independent raw-source arithmetic verifies the actual binary64 numerator and
the exact target copy/BIL identity. No forecast/risk fit, source ranking or
original target builder is rerun.

On a scored date, participate only when the recorded selected pair's smaller
SPY/QQQ forecast daily active rate, after the **retained advisor's** nominal
switch proxy divided by21, is strictly positive. Use the exact saved target,
not another pair or newly fitted weight. Negative and zero cases use exactly
98%BIL and2%idle cash. Before score maturity, use the same explicitly unscored
BIL funding standby, not the old equal-eight initial intention. Preserve every
formal return date. A missing, future, inconsistent or nonfinite mature
certificate is an error, never a successful cash fallback.

Funding standby is a capital mode, not a ninth strategy expert. It does not
reclassify any source as technically invalid. Original global rosters and
admission scope stay unchanged. No hierarchical expert admission or fabricated
pre-initial opinion is required; the genuine main initialization clock remains.

## Statistical state is not main holdings

Participation and main BIL holdings do **not** feed back into the retained
advisor. Its nominal cost proxy therefore is not the new main account's actual
cost forecast. Its numerator is not promised future main NET return. The new
account separately charges actual original fees, delay and holdings feedback;
source shadow wealth/fees are not summed or transferred as capital.
