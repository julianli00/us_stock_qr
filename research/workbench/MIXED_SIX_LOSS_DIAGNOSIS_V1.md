# Saved mixed-six losses: read-only diagnosis V1

This source-scoped diagnosis reads the eight already completed mixed-mechanism-six
main accounts. Both complete policies failed their original four-path contract.
It adds no financial configuration, expert, target, shadow, main account or BT run.
The earlier pair-score persistence question already exists and is not repeated.
The adjusted-unit PnL identity and exact stored-episode comparison have prior art;
they are reused, not presented as a new independent investment strategy.

The small ledger Module's Interface is `decompose(account, weights, open, close)`
and `interval(daily, start, stop)`. It validates the full saved cash-funded book,
reconstructs adjusted model units from actual end-close weights and NAV, and
reconciles previous-unit overnight marks, current-unit intraday marks and recorded
fees to observed daily NAV changes. Cash-flow and closing cash identities are
checked separately; vector sums are compared with scalar `math.fsum`.
These model units are not broker shares or an inferred source expert allocation.

Every original2512/1254-day lattice remains. Initial cash and the running DD peak
are USD10000, not the first observed NAV after costs. The earliest exact maximum
observed DD is chosen; its peak is the latest preceding exact running maximum,
including initial capital. A zero-DD book keeps an explicit absent episode.
All fourteen assets remain, including zero and positive offset contributions.
Recorded BIL is a price-bearing ETF; idle cash earns no invented interest.

Monetary reconciliation is fixed at USD1e-6 and stored-DD agreement at1e-10 before
new outcomes. These are diagnostic accounting checks, not changed financial gates
or a tolerance search. The canonical `review_market` reads the same frozen price
units and original RF. Saved account CSVs retain17-digit round-trip parsing.
No new quote request, normalization, missing-date fill or source waiver follows.

For each of four original path labels, the two rules are also observed on both
rules' own exact peak-to-trough close clocks. These are already-held interval NAV
changes, not fresh capital, replacement drawdowns, shorter financial windows or
new acceptance tests. Both whole paths and their original criteria remain.

Per-asset marks and fees sum to the actual episode loss. A fee fraction divides
recorded interval fees by that actual net loss only when the interval is a loss;
otherwise it is explicitly undefined. The split is an accounting identity, not
causal expert alpha or a simulated fee-free policy. Removing costs could change
future holdings and peaks, so no fee-free strategy or counterfactual DD is claimed.

The source selection is retrospective after271 exposed financial completions.
No independence, forward validation or automatic next policy follows. One exact
source/code/test freeze precedes one streamed actual pass; a real failure retains
partial evidence and closes the identity without repairing frozen law. Publish
only aggregate own-book PnL, fees and date-clock evidence, never raw prices,
adjusted units, targets, daily rows or private paths' contents.
