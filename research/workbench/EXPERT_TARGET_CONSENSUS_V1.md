# Complete-expert recommendation consensus V1

This independently selected November question changes the **allocation
operator**, not the seven admitted experts, sources or final financial gates.
Can robust agreement among current complete recommendations replace noisy
return-score ranking without fitting another return predictor?

At the original initial/month-end decisions, every expert has one vote.
Append residual idle cash to each full underlying-asset recommendation.
Choose every expert whose total L1 distance to all seven recommendations is
minimal, and split capital equally across exact ties. The result remains a
mixture of complete policies, not coordinatewise asset-sleeve selection.
The primary implementation compares exact rational values of original
binary64 requests; an independent integer-coordinate oracle checks its choice
and combined target. There is no fitted parameter, norm scan, solver or
epsilon tie.

The rule uses only recommendations already known at the decision. It does not
rank, fill or call immature NET-return scores successful. Original source
availability, NET calendars, exact RF identity and admission remain required.
Missing or invalid inputs stop the version. All observations, target binary64
values and actual introduction clocks remain separate from main capital.

L1 opinion distance is **not actual turnover, calibrated dependence, a risk
forecast or a drawdown guarantee**. The preserved monthly actual-holdings
execution, cash funding, costs, delays and all four original qualification
paths remain decisive. Stored rolling-seven is the primary control; equal-seven
is separately labeled context. Neither old control is rerun.

The directional robustness premise is unsupported unless every new path lowers
primary-control drawdown without increasing annualized actual turnover.
This is a research diagnostic, not an extra or substitute user gate. The
original joint Sharpe/CAGR/drawdown/SPY/QQQ gates still alone determine financial
qualification. Exposed historical selection is not independent OOS or forward
proof, and seven experts are not seven independent factors.

The first stage freezes this economic law and its synthetic-checked aggregation
implementation **before any new real consensus targets or main accounts**.
It is not a financial registration, completed capital evaluation or admission
of the blocked term expert. Orchestration, complete dependency freeze and an
actual preregistration publication fence must precede funded evaluation.
