# Stored pair score persistence diagnosis V1

This fixed zero-financial-trial diagnosis compares original-eight past pair-score
ordering with the following complete exchange month's source assessment.
The persistence interpretation is introduced here: the original lower
train/validation score was a selection heuristic, not a calibrated next-month
return or Sharpe forecast.

The earlier objective-alignment diagnosis assessed selected-source benchmark
alignment and executed main intervals. Contest-credit diagnosis assessed expert
credit proportions. Pair-risk diagnosis assessed variance, correlation and ERC
stability. None is replayed. This node reuses the latter's already saved
input/resolved binary64 future observations and stored future variance.

All121 original decision states remain, including seven unscored origins and
all28 pairs at114 mature origins. Only the113 complete following exchange months
are assessed. The final September30 origin requires October30, outside the
original source books ending October5; all28 final pairs remain censored.
No partial-month statistic, arbitrary21-row slice or newer snapshot is used.

For each positive-risk complete pair, scalar mean source NET-minus-RF is formed
using only its already recorded fixed weights. The statistical outcome uses
the original recorded incremental switching proxy divided by21 and the square
root of already stored future portfolio variance, with the original annualizing
convention. NumPy and scalar-fsum calculations are independently compared.
No covariance, variance, past score, choice, target or account is rerun.

Native NET already contains source costs. The additional proxy is a reference
assessment convention, not actual future paid fees. The outcome is not a
funded portfolio, main capital, transferable shadow wealth or a newly observed
main-account Sharpe. Exact constant/undefined future risk stays unscorable;
no variance floor or RF normalization is allowed.

The primary statistic is the actual original choice's future midrank percentile
among evaluable pairs, retaining half credit for exact ties. An all-tied month
has percentile0.5. Undefined selected outcomes retain an explicit missing
assessment, not a successful rank. The secondary statistic is monthly Kendall
tau-b between original12-decimal past keys and future reference assessments,
with exact ties and undefined denominators retained.

Ranks are descriptive checks of an original decision. They do not form an
alternative chooser, an ex-post best portfolio, a new financial acceptance gate
or a causal source-profit attribution. No p-values, confidence claims, new
parameters, windows, bins, experts or pool composition follow.

The question is selected after249 exposed completed configurations and known
previous results. Shared experts and overlapping training periods are dependent;
historical future maturity is not new independent OOS or real forward proof.
All failures, original source scopes, rosters and financial rejections remain.
One exact source/code/test freeze precedes one streamed actual pass; a real
failure closes the identity with partial evidence rather than repair/retry.
