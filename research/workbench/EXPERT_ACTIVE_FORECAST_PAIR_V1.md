# Saved active-return forecasts: a separate funded pair study V1

This study asks whether the previously saved monetary-rate assessments can
support a useful **main portfolio**, not whether the contextual diagnostic has
won. Both original forecasters are included, without selecting a model or expert
from complete errors. The eight complete admitted source policies, their actual
introduction/admission clocks, fourteen assets and global rosters stay fixed.
The existing equal-eight and original rolling accounts are cached controls,
not new trials.

The prior diagnosis had small mixed descriptive skill, no allocation and no
financial qualification. Its two unit-ridge laws, fixed penalty and expanding
monthly learning are not changed, rerun or refitted. A forecast is an input to
allocation, not a ninth strategy expert. The cached-source and synthetic
Adapters cross the same `allocate` Interface. Frozen recorded observations are
joined by exact identity, date, NET/RF values and information clock.

## New law, frozen before allocations

For each mature date, retain every one of the original 28 complete-expert risk
pairs, including explicit unscorable entries. Copy each original 84-session
RF-excess-trained ERC weight vector unchanged. Training covariance dependence,
correlation and marginal variance contribution are retained and independently
checked; do not fit another pair-weight model or rerun the source selector.

Each new policy owns its previous **statistical** expert weights. Compare its
current-opinion underlying target with every candidate pair target. Keep the
original nominal 5bp two-sided L1 change plus USD1 per changed asset, at USD10000,
with the inherited 1e-12 change threshold and 21-session amortization. This is
not the main account's actual holdings or a promise of actual costs.

For each pair and each saved predictor, weight its eight forecast daily active
rates against both original NET SPY and QQQ references. Subtract the nominal
switch proxy per day. Divide the resulting daily numerator separately by the
original 84-session and 42-session RF-excess pair standard deviations and
annualize by sqrt252. Rank by the minimum of all four scores, rounded to the
original 12 decimal places, with lexical pair ties.

The denominator is **RF-excess pair risk**, not active tracking risk. These past
risk windows are not held-out validation of the expanding predictor. The
forecast numerator is a source assessment rate, not future realized main NET
return. Subtracting a common benchmark is not a pair-specific new alpha source
or two independent predictions of economic risk.

Negative finite forecasts still rank; there is no new cash cutoff, clipping,
ridge tuning, variance floor or safety guarantee. Before the original common
126 observations, copy the exact original unscored equal-eight intention.
A missing mature prediction stops the version rather than becoming equal
weights. An originally unscorable risk menu remains explicitly unsuccessful.
