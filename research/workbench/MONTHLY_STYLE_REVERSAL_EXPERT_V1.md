## Fixed economic question

clock, measure each of MTUM, VLUE, QUAL, USMV, IJR and PKW over exactly21 completed
sessions, using22 admitted total-return close levels. Rank ascending by the
simple price ratio minus one, rounded to12decimal places, then lexical symbol.
Recommend49%capital in each of the two relatively weakest styles, with2%idle
cash and zero in every other admitted asset. This is a complete monthly
contrarian policy, not an ETF sleeve renamed as an expert.

The premise is temporary relative style-flow pressure followed by recovery.
It differs from chasing recent strength, price-trend defense, option signals,
hedge covariance, credit information and calendar participation. A one-month
signal and monthly commitment align that premise with the existing low-frequency
execution; the bottom-two basket is one fixed sparse research choice.
There is no window, count, cutoff, weight, execution or band search.

Relative weakness can persist. Both selected equity styles can fall with the
market; different behavior is not independent risk or a future hedge.
Concentration, holding drift, delayed execution, actual turnover, model tickets
and fees can defeat the premise. No numeric turnover cap or gain is assumed.

price window at a declared position. It returns a complete fourteen-asset
opinion and its resolved assessment. It rejects insufficient history,
nonmonth-end clocks and invalid source-ratio arithmetic. No opinion exists
until22consecutive actual price levels are available; missing or invalid data
does not become BIL, idle safety or a successful score.

native month-end opinions, and can retain the22dates and binary64source prices

unavailable, rather than filled recommendations. The source must have a known
following session; the final covered calendar date is not guessed to be month end.
