# Stored macro extension state diagnosis V1

This fixed, zero-financial-trial audit compares the completed original-eight
and scoped-macro-nine decision records. It asks whether adding a source changed
later choices among the original sources even when the current chosen pair
contains no macro source. It does not repair either rejected financial rule.

The earlier tail-cost diagnosis compared nominal changes with actual paid fees.
The earlier pair-risk diagnosis compared recorded risk with a subsequent full
month. Neither is repeated here: this audit compares the two recorded learning
paths and their shared pair-score inputs.

All121 original input/resolved states are retained, including seven immature
states. Every shared source observation must be binary64-identical in both
records, including NET, RF, recommendation, dates, identity and signed zero.
Each mature origin retains all28 shared pair records and all8 additional macro
pair records. The original source/introduction and historical availability
meanings remain unchanged.

For already-scored shared pairs, recorded weights can differ numerically across
the eight- and nine-dimensional calculations; those differences are reported,
not normalized away. Original84/42 source-NET-minus-RF moments and recorded
incremental switching penalties algebraically reconcile the recorded score gap:
the source-reference component plus the switching-proxy component plus the
floating-point residual. The existing independent scalar-score tolerance1e-8
applies only to reconstruction of already-recorded scores. Shared inputs and RF
are not tolerance-matched. No variance floor or replacement assessment view is
allowed.

Source NET already includes native shadow costs. A reference moment without the
additional switching penalty is neither gross returns nor a fee-free funded
portfolio. The switching proxy uses nominal recommendations and the preceding
expert allocation, not actual main holdings, requests, tickets or paid fees.
No new candidate ranking, chosen weights, targets, source wealth or main account
is produced.

Recorded choices are classified as current macro inclusion, the same original
pair, or a different original pair with zero current macro allocation.
Preceding macro mass and exact preceding-state agreement are recorded separately.
An earlier allocation can affect a later switching reference without current
macro exposure. This observation is not a controlled counterfactual or causal
attribution of actual CAGR, Sharpe, fees or drawdown.

The question was selected after249 exposed completed configurations, both
completed financial studies and the known macro-selection count. Overlapping
months and pair cases are not independent samples or prospective validation.
Descriptive medians, p90s and maxima choose no new parameter, source or policy.
Original active-seven/selected-eight rosters, limited monthly macro admission,
native RF incompatibility and all financial rejections remain unchanged.

Exact design, dependencies, inputs and successful synthetic validation are
frozen before new arithmetic. One streamed pass is allowed. A real missing or
changed record terminates this identity with partial evidence retained; it does
not trigger a schema, precision, source or Adapter rescue.
