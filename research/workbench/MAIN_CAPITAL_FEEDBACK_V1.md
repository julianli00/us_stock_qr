# Actual main-capital feedback V1

## Frozen economic question

The original eight-expert rolling rule prices switching using changes between
nominal current expert-opinion targets at USD 10000. The separately frozen
close-mark cost Module now has two tested Adapters. This study asks whether
using the main account's legitimately known dollar holdings, cash and current
NAV in that cost input yields a useful complete portfolio policy.

There is one new financial rule, not a score/window/band/cost sweep. The original
eight study representatives, mechanisms and source-admission scopes are fixed.
Credit, Macro, HMM/VXN and unadmitted FXY are not added or automatically promoted.
The original eight equal and rolling results are cached context controls and
are not replayed. A comparison is a whole-policy contrast, not isolated causal
credit alpha, fee savings or proof of independence.

## Capital-state and decision clock

`DecisionPolicy.decide(CapitalSnapshot)` is the Interface at the chronological
main-account decision Seam. `CloseMarkedPairPolicy` is the actual rolling
Adapter. `ArchivedOpinionPolicy` supplies a genuinely different existing
monthly-opinion Adapter for synthetic equivalence tests; it is not another
financial candidate or a new admitted expert.

Every formal path starts in fresh USD 10000 cash, with a new allocation learning
state and the original preceding-session capital anchor. A new meta assessment
at that real anchor consumes the then-known source opinions and matured shadow
NET/RF observations. This is explicitly a path-local capital-initialization
assessment, not a backdated ordinary month-end recommendation or an assumption
that a closed-loop main account existed before funding.

In particular, the five-year anchor uses information known on 2021-10-05.
The cached old five-year control instead seeded its carried precomputed meta
opinion. This necessary initialization distinction is disclosed; comparisons
with that cached control do not identify a pure cost-input treatment effect.
No initial return, execution delay, capital, RF or drawdown peak is changed.

After initialization, assess only genuine exchange month ends after the current
close and the pool's admitted after-16:15-ET information clock. Obtain complete
expert observations through that day only. Retain all original 126/84/42
maturity, lower train/validation net risk score, equal marginal variance pair
weights, mechanism exclusions, 21-session cost amortization and 12dp/lexical
tie rules. Missing sources fail explicitly; unscored/degenerate histories keep
the original explicitly unscored equal weights without safety claims.

The score's close-marked funding estimate always uses the frozen baseline
5bps/USD 1 convention, including in stress. It prices the whole candidate
physical rebalance and the original 2.5pp actual-holdings band. It is not the
future opening execution price or cost. Stored shadow NET already includes
frictions; the extra penalty can double-penalize some behavior and is not a
calibrated return forecast.

Emit one unified 98% ETF target. Apply the unchanged actual-holdings band using
the current main book; if an execution is issued, fill only at the original
later one/two-session opening with actual 5/20bps plus USD 1 modeled tickets.
Charge those fills once, not the score penalty. Keep cash funding, long-only
fractional quantities, no idle-cash interest, and BIL as an actual ETF.
New complete-target and conservation postconditions reject an inconsistent
fixed-commission solver result without patching the original solver. Its
original rounding-only no-op remains a no-op.

## Dynamic-program publication version

Precomputed targets cannot be generated by secretly simulating the new main
capital before preregistration. The explicitly versioned packet instead freezes
a causal decision program, complete admitted source inputs, synthetic Interface
proof, exact parameters, implementation, dependencies and all acceptance gates.
It registers one actual identity and must be actually published before the
first historical new meta score, target or capital output.

Program readiness is labeled synthetic/read-only-input readiness, not a real
historical target-derivation proof. Real target proof is recorded after the
published program has actually executed and its outputs have been checked.
Old precomputed-target packets, their event order and historical criteria are
not edited or retrospectively reclassified.

Only after that fence, execute all four original full formal paths, retaining
private chronological decision/capital traces and partial failures. Rebuild
the issued requests with the original independent MIT bt accounting and metric
audit, and independently verify actual holding gaps, delays and the main
dollar state supplied to every decision. Five fixed ten-year-base prefix
replays at return positions 125, 503, 1006, 1509 and 2012 are separately retained
auxiliary causality audits, not extra economic rules or qualifying windows.
They occur after publication, never during preparation. Synthetic future-price
perturbation tests complement them; neither expands source/PIT admission.

## Unchanged acceptance and limitations

Retain full 10y/5y windows, 2512/1254 returns, USD 10000 initial peak, exact RF,
cash funding and all original financial gates on every path: strict net
Sharpe > 1, net CAGR > 12%, DD <= 15%, matched-cost full-capital tradable SPY and
QQQ and original extra gates. Disclose real model turnover, tickets and fees.
15%/20% CAGR preferences do not replace hard gates; no numeric turnover ceiling
is invented. Synthetic tests or technical expert validity are not qualification.

Expert identities and the new meta policy are actually introduced in November.
All formal history is exposed, counterfactual and not independent forward
validation. No source requests, source generator/fits/shadow account replays,
source/RF view changes, FXY statutory-source rescue or brokerage operations are
part of this study. Preserve every old failed policy and all new partial
technical/financial outcomes.
