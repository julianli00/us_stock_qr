## Frozen arithmetic

Remove the equal-six common coordinate mean from NET logs and negative loss
labels. Project the latter exactly onto the natural-log utility ray and its
orthogonal remainder. At a finite stored rate, utility displacement equals
the parallel scaling displacement plus the rate-scaled orthogonal remainder.
Their squared norms add exactly. Absolute L1 components can cancel and are
not additive percentage shares.

Centering removes only a per-month common offset. Rates are read, not
recalculated from gaps; old history is not refiltered and no posterior is
generated. Independent160-digit Decimal arithmetic checks exact Fractions
of the stored decimal and binary64 operands at unchanged1e-12.
An affine-ray assessment at that limit is a descriptive result, not an
acceptance condition that must be forced to pass. Preserve nonaffine months,
rank reversals and created/broken ties.

At each saved origin, compare relative L1 magnitudes across its already-known
months. A common finite positive rate cancels from these magnitude shares.
It does **not** cancel from the source posterior with its switching prior.
These shares are not posterior importance, marginal portfolio risk or
calendar probabilities. Untrained origins and analytic-infinite constant-loss
histories remain explicit; zero magnitude has undefined shares rather than
uniform or zero fallback shares.
