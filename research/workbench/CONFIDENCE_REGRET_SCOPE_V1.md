# Saved confidence-regret scope V1

This zero-trial diagnosis does not replace the completed Prod learner, rerun its
targets or accounts, change participation ratings, or fit another allocation.
It asks what the saved conditional source-game comparator measures, and how the
prior rating coincides with actual later main-account loss months.

The pure inspection Module is the Seam between saved source-game/account rows
and descriptive aggregates. The real archived-record Adapter and synthetic
Adapters use the same Interface. No learner `weights`, `update`, source
generator, account or old diagnosis entrypoint is invoked.

For each mature source month and each of the six experts, take the played
loss from its saved high-precision decimal string and the loss/rating from
their exact saved binary64 values. Decompose plain played-minus-source excess
into rating-weighted excess plus one-minus-rating-weighted excess. These are
signed exact Fractions. Positive excess means the learner lost more than that
expert in the source game; negative excess means less. A smaller magnitude
under partial participation is not automatically better financial performance.

Compare exact reconstructed weighted excess and cumulative sums with recorded
rounded excess/regret under the unchanged 1e-12 coherence limit. Do not repair
old states or re-run the learner to obtain different weights. Inspect every
one of the 121 decisions and all 119 mature source months. The two initial
unscored observations cannot acquire fabricated losses.

The rating held at the prior source-month origin is the relevant rating for
that month's regret. End-month ratings cannot retroactively select losses.
Use only exact zero, strictly partial, and exact one participation groups:
there are no fitted thresholds, quantiles, chosen episodes or new score.
Confidence is not a calibrated probability or technical admission decision.

Separately, compound each saved main book's binary64 NET returns over complete
matched source months, using exact rational relative products for loss signs.
This is a descriptive statistic of an existing book, not a new account,
annual metric, fee-free counterfactual or source-to-main regret conversion.
Keep all four paths and original 7532 formal rows. The ten-year books contain
119 full source months each; the fresh five-year books contain 59 each.
Cross-initialization and terminal boundary days remain explicitly retained.
They still contribute to the original financial verdict and its initial fees.

Report loss-month counts by prior rating group and by later rating-change
direction. These retrospective associations do not demonstrate causality,
forecast calibration, a policy that knew the later rating earlier, or
independent samples. Repeated horizons and cost scenarios overlap.
Source-game regret and actual main wealth remain different quantities.

Freeze criteria, source hashes, code and successful synthetic evidence, then
save and prospectively link an actual preanalysis checkpoint before new labels.
Retain every diagnostic input before inspection and resolved output afterward:
242 source-game records, 712 complete-main-month records and eight boundary
records, 962 total. Five retained-input prefixes must remain exact.
Only aggregate diagnostic summaries are public; raw ratings, source losses,
NET rows and dated month cases remain private on the SSD.

Financial J1490, the rejected four-path result, all old failed versions,
original source admission/RF/clock restrictions and user-owned files stay
unchanged. No new investment qualification or independent forward validation
follows this diagnosis.
