# Worst-month expert opportunity selection V1

This independently frozen question addresses uncertainty about which complete
policy will outperform, not a repair of covariance forecasts, a risk-cap scan
or another membership change. Use the same eight admitted complete experts,
including the conditional hedge source. The original active seven policy,
the rejected eight-source controls and the local Macro compatibility remain
unchanged. No ninth representative is introduced.

The action menu contains all 28 unordered source pairs, with exactly half of
the statistical allocation in each member. For every genuinely complete,
already matured exchange month, compound each source's native NET observations.
The reference is the largest of the eight month relatives. A pair's relative
is the arithmetic mean of its two source relatives: an equal-initial-capital
statistical source mixture, not daily rebalancing or a funded main account.
Its opportunity loss is the logarithm of reference divided by pair relative.

At each decision, rank the worst opportunity loss across all matured months,
plus a one-time current-switch log penalty. The penalty is minus log1p of
negative proxy cost divided by USD10000. The proxy uses the original base
5bps per side and USD1 per changed asset coordinate, applying new-minus-previous
expert fractions to current source opinions. It is not actual main holdings,
total turnover, scalable native fixed fees or a second debit to main capital.
Round the score to the original 12 decimal places, then use sorted stable
expert identifiers for ties. There is no solver, confidence bound, forgetting,
temperature, variance floor or tuned risk threshold.

Keep the original initial capital decision, every exchange month-end decision
and every formal return date. The initial incomplete source month is not used.
The initial anchor and 2016-10-31 are explicitly unscored equal eighths;
2016-11-30 is the first possible full-month assessment. Memory expands over
all completed intervals, at most 119, without resetting for the five-year
account. Partial October 2026 is not assimilated. The 2512-observation source
delivery request is not the maturity requirement for this different score.

The small rule Interface consumes admitted policy opinions and native
assessment observations. The same caller/test seam emits one underlying-asset
target and an audit, with raw exact binary64 inputs retained before new
calculations and resolved fields afterward. Missing identity, opinion, date
or common RF is an explicit error, never successful immaturity. Every mature
decision retains all 28 pair assessments, not only its winner.

Freeze the full implementation, dependencies and successful canonical checks
before new source-month calculations. Actual new registration precedes scores
and targets; remote preregistration precedes funding. Reuse unchanged execution,
costs, delays, RF, initial DD peak, fresh USD10000 and full four-path SPY/QQQ
qualification. Actual turnover, tickets and fees must be reported.

Source NET books include their own fees and intramonth policy feedback.
Monthly aggregation does not copy that feedback into main capital or transfer
wealth/protection guarantees. Worst historical opportunity loss is neither
future regret nor a loss bound. All prior histories and 238 completed
configurations are exposed; selection is not independent OOS or real forward
proof. Cached equal-eight and rolling-eight results are context only and are
not rerun. No newer synchronized inputs or missed prospective days are spliced.
